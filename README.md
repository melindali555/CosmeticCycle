# Background Information
As makeup products have become an increasingly important part of modern life, excessive consumption and infrequent use have contributed to the waste of expired cosmetics. Consumers may be left with products that they cannot finish using.
Using expired cosmetics on the skin may pose health risks due to potential product deterioration or microbial contamination. Meanwhile throwing them away can contribute to environmental damage.

CosmeticCycle explores an alternative approach by using AI to help users discover creative ways to repurpose expired makeup into drawing materials and pigments. Instead of treating expired cosmetics as waste or
continuing to use them on the skin, the project encourages users to transform them into materials for artistic creation through personalized, AI-generated suggestions. By turning waste reduction into a hands-on and creative experience, 
CosmeticCycle aims to make sustainability more engaging and accessible, allowing users to participate in environmental action while exploring their creativity. 
At the same time, the project raises awareness of the importance of avoiding the use of expired cosmetics on the skin. 

# Features
- Accepts cosmetic product information and product condition details.
- Allows users to provide ingredient information by pasting text or uploading images of the ingredient table.
- Uses AI to generate personalized suggestions for repurposing expired cosmetics as drawing materials and pigments.
- Incorporates findings from prior experiments on transforming selected cosmetics into pigments.

# Disclaimer
AI-generated suggestions are exploratory and do not guarantee successful results or the safety of expired cosmetic to reuse. 
Expired cosmetics showing visible mold, unusual odors, or other abnormal changes should not be reused. Experimental findings apply only to the specific products and condition tested.
